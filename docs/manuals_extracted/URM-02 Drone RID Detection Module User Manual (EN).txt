1.ProductOverview
TheURM-02dronedetectionenhancementmodulereceivesanddecodesbroadcast
messagesignalstransmittedremotelybydrones.Dronescompliantwiththefollowing
internationalstandardscanbedetectedandidentified:ChinaNationalStandardsGB42590-
2023andGB46750-2025;EuropeanstandardASD-STANDINEN4709-002;andU.S.ASTMRemote
IDstandardASTMF3411-2a-RID-BF3586-22.Themoduleintegratesabuilt-inGNSSpositioning
moduleandanLNA(LowNoiseAmplifier),withanoptional4Gcommunicationmodule.Itoffers
comprehensiveexternalinterfaces:astandardRJ45Ethernetportforcommunication,a
standardDCpowerinput,aType-Cportforpoweranddebugging,andareservedGPIO
expansioninterface.DetectionresultdataisoutputviatheEthernetport(anoptional4G
modulewithMQTTprotocoltransmissionisavailable).

4.FunctionalDescription
4.1AlarmDataOutputviaEthernetPort(UDPCommunication)
(1)Poweronthedevice:UsetheDCpowerportortheType-Cporttopoweron.
(2)Connectthedevice:UseanEthernetcabletoconnectthedevicedirectlytoacomputer.
(3)Setthecomputer'slocalIPaddress:ConfiguretheIPaddressofthecorresponding
Ethernetinterfaceonthecomputerto192.168.0.X(thedevice'sdefaultIPis192.168.0.3,soX
cannotbe3),forexample192.168.0.100,andsetthesubnetmaskto255.255.255.0.Asshownin
thefigurebelow.

(4)Configurethenetworkdebuggingutility:OpenNetAssist.exe,selectUDPastheprotocol
type,setthelocalhostaddresstotheIPaddressconfiguredinstep(3),e.g.,192.168.0.100,set
thelocalhostportto65100,andclick"Open".

(5)Viewdevicelocationinformation:Whennodroneisdetected,thedevicecontinuously
outputsitsownGNSSlatitudeandlongitude.
(6)Viewdronealarminformation:Onceadroneisdetected,thecorrespondingdronealarm
informationwillbeoutput.
