1.ProductOverview
TheURM-01droneRIDdetectionmodulereceivesanddecodesbroadcastmessagesignals
transmittedremotelybydrones.Dronescompliantwiththefollowinginternationalstandards
canbedetectedandidentified:ChinaNationalStandardsGB42590-2023andGB46750-2025;
EuropeanstandardASD-STANDINEN4709-002;andU.S.ASTMRemoteIDstandardASTMF3411-
2a-RID-BF3586-22.DetectionresultdataisoutputviatheType-CinterfaceorSH1.0_4pin
connector.
